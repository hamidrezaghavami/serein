## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the philosophy of quiet luxury, meditative minimalism, and artisanal restraint tailored for high-end independent skincare. It strips away standard e-commerce clutter—such as flashing sale badges, countdown timers, and dense product grids—in favor of spacious, editorial layouts inspired by luxury art catalogs and architectural monographs.

The target audience consists of discerning, design-literate consumers who view daily skincare as an intentional, sensory ritual. Visual cues honor natural travertine stone, frosted matte glass, and soft directional daylight. The aesthetic is clean, warm, grounding, and deliberate, relying on structural simplicity, exacting typography, and generous negative space to evoke stillness and tactile luxury.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from physical materiality: warm ivory plaster, aged limestone, muted botanical sage, and deep mineral charcoal.

- **Primary (`#292D29`):** Deep charcoal used for precise typography, high-contrast actions, key icon strokes, and prominent borders. It ensures razor-sharp legibility without the harsh coldness of pure `#000000`.
- **Secondary (`#A8B2A0`):** Muted botanical sage, providing a subtle organic counterpoint for accent states, selection highlights, active indicators, and botanical ingredient callouts.
- **Tertiary (`#D8CCBC`):** Pale warm sand used predominantly for delicate hairline dividers, subtle surface fills, and gentle boundary outlines.
- **Neutral (`#F5F2EB`):** Warm ivory foundation serving as the primary ambient canvas, preventing clinical starkness. Pure soft white (`#FFFFFF`) and translucent warm white (`#FAF8F5`) are applied selectively to product card containers and floating sheets.

## Typography

The typography pairs an authoritative, high-end editorial serif (`Newsreader`) with a clean, humanist geometric sans-serif (`Plus Jakarta Sans`).

- **Headlines & Editorial Titles:** Rendered in `Newsreader` regular or italic weights, lending the tone of an independent art publication. Headlines should never use heavy bold weights; structural hierarchy is achieved through scale and breathing room.
- **Body & Continuous Reading:** Formatted in `Plus Jakarta Sans` with relaxed line-heights for effortless legibility against ivory surfaces.
- **Micro-copy, Metadata, & Action Labels:** Rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` medium or semibold, often styled with subtle uppercase tracking to reflect bottle silkscreen printing and laboratory numbering (e.g., `01`, `BALANCING TONER`, `50 ML / 1.7 fl oz`).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an intentional, fluid 12-column grid system (4-column on mobile, 8-column on tablet) calibrated for asymmetrical storytelling and generous whitespace.

- **Margins & Gutters:** Desktop experiences utilize generous outer margins (`margin-desktop: 4.5rem`) to create a gallery-like perimeter. Column gutters range from `1.5rem` on mobile to `2.5rem` on widescreen displays to give photographic assets and textual passages independence.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Sections breathe through large vertical gaps (`space-xl`), intentionally pacing the scroll to match a calm, unhurried mindset.
- **Asymmetry & Composition:** Product details frequently juxtapose full-bleed or large-format imagery on one side with slender, structured text columns on the other, anchored by consistent grid baselines.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids drop shadows, blurred ambient glows, and artificial skeuomorphic elevations. Depth is established strictly through planar hierarchy, tonal layering, and fine hairline boundaries:

- **Tonal Layering:** The global viewport rests on `#F5F2EB`. Cards, drawers, and modular focus zones elevate by shifting to `#FFFFFF` or translucent `#FAF8F5`.
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Spatial separation is punctuated by delicate `1px` borders colored in `#D8CCBC` with 40% to 60% opacity. These clean lines segment navigation bars, table cells, and module borders without adding visual mass.
- **Frosted Glass (Backdrop Filter):** Sticky navigation bars and floating overlays utilize high-diffusion backdrops (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background-color: rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.85)`), echoing the frosted glass packaging of the bottles.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a sharp, architectural standard (`roundedness: 0`). Elements honor clean right angles and geometric precision:

- Buttons, inputs, product media frames, cards, and modal sheets have `0px` border-radius by default, reinforcing an editorial, museum-grade aesthetic.
- A maximum border-radius of `2px` is permitted only on subtle interaction elements (e.g., custom checkboxes or miniature tags) when anti-aliasing softens the hard corner.
- Circular elements are strictly reserved for functional round signifiers, such as color shade selectors or circular photo stamps.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#292D29` fill with `#F5F2EB` text, rectangular (`0px` radius), uppercase `label-md` lettering with `0.08em` tracking. Padding: `14px 32px`. Hover transitions smoothly to opacity 88% or subtle tint.
- **Secondary / Ghost Action:** Transparent background with a `1px solid #292D29` boundary. Hover fills subtly with `#292D29` and turns text `#F5F2EB`.
- **Text Link:** Understated body text with an offset `1px solid currentColor` baseline underline that animates on hover.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean flat container using `#FFFFFF` or `#FAF8F5`, enclosed by a delicate `1px solid rgba(216, 204, 188, 0.45)` border.
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratio 1:1 or 4:5 with serene, warm-neutral backdrops. 
- **Content:** Product category/numbering in `label-sm` (`#A8B2A0`), product name in `headline-sm` serif, subtitle and volume in `body-sm`, followed by price in `label-lg`. No loud "SALE" or "HOT" badges; out-of-stock items feature a restrained, muted status line.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Minimalist underline or 4-sided `1px solid #D8CCBC` frame. Background `#FAF8F5` or transparent. Placeholder text in `#292D29` at 40% opacity. Focus state deepens border to `#292D29` with zero shadow ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp square indicators (`0px` radius) with `1px solid #292D29` borders. Filled state uses a centered micro-square or solid `#292D29` fill.

### Chips & Filters
- **Appearance:** Low-profile rectangular chips bordered with `1px solid #D8CCBC`. Selected state shifts background to `#A8B2A0` with `#FFFFFF` text or solid `#292D29` depending on prominence.

### Ingredient & Formula Drawers
- **Accordion / Tabs:** Clean, border-separated horizontal rows (`1px solid rgba(216, 204, 188, 0.5)`). Expand/collapse icons are delicate `+` and `−` symbols using `1px` stroke weights.
